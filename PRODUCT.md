# Neon Knockout 3D

A browser arena brawler for friends playing over the same Wi-Fi. One computer hosts an authoritative Node server; two to eight players join its local address. The user explicitly requested a separate repository named neon-knockout-3d, a Three.js 2.5D presentation, improved animation, and four fighters with different appearances and abilities. Turkish is the product language. Preserve the established short ring-out matches, lobby, invitations, reconnects, touch controls, and keyboard controls.

Players should recognize a fighter by silhouette and movement before reading its name. RIFT is an athletic blade-like striker, BASTION a broad armored brawler, PULSE a floating reactor with orbital machinery, and WRAITH a slim spectral hooded machine. The gameplay stays on a flat plane; the camera is fixed above and behind the arena, and height is visual only. No account, internet service, external asset CDN, or matchmaking is needed to play.

The existing source repository is not edited. This repository derives from upstream main at 4a4e7887a6a28cc2aab8d45efc4a53f3f6efaca3. This first release includes character-specific movement abilities on the existing Space/touch dash input; team modes and other brainstormed ideas are outside this release.
